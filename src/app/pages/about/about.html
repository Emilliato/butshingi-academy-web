About the Academy

Rooted in Mqonci. Building futures across the Chris Hani District.

S. Butshingi Academy is a registered NPO (Reg 2020/119065/07) giving learners access to quality education, appropriate resources and real developmental opportunity.

Our vision

Confident, skilled, responsible young people.

To become a leading community-based institution that develops confident, skilled and responsible young people.

Our mission

Every learner reaching their full potential.

To provide learners with quality education, appropriate learning resources, technology, life skills and developmental opportunities that enable them to reach their full potential.
