Privacy & POPIA

Privacy Policy

How S. Butshingi Academy collects, uses and protects personal information — including photos of our learners and staff — in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. Who we are

S. Butshingi Academy ("the Academy", "we", "us") is a registered NPO (Reg 2020/119065/07) based in Mqonci, Chris Hani District, Eastern Cape. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information through this website, our admissions process, and our use of photos and media of learners, staff and visitors.

2. Information we collect

  • Admissions & enquiry information: learner details (name, date of birth, gender, grade, home language, previous school), guardian details (name, relationship, phone, email, address), and any information you choose to share with us (e.g. special needs, motivation).
  • Contact form submissions: your name, email address and message.
  • Photos and video: images of learners, staff and school events, used on this website, our social media and printed materials.
  • Staff account information: for authorised staff using the admin panel to manage applications and news.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal information to third parties.

3. Children's information

Much of the information we collect relates to minors. Under POPIA, we only process a child's personal information — including photos — with the consent of a parent or legal guardian ("a competent person"), unless a specific legal exception applies. By submitting an admissions application, a parent or guardian consents to us processing the learner's information for the purpose of that application. Consent for photos and media is handled separately and is always optional — see section 6 below.

4. Why we process this information

  • To process and manage admissions applications
  • To respond to enquiries made via our contact form
  • To communicate with guardians about placement, orientation and school matters
  • To celebrate learner achievements and share school news, with consent, via photos and stories
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and reporting obligations as a registered NPO school

5. Photos & media

We ask parents and guardians to complete a separate Photo & Media Consent Form before any photo or video of their child is used on our website, social media or in printed materials. This consent:

  • Is entirely optional and is never a condition of admission
  • Can be limited (e.g. website only, not social media) or withdrawn at any time
  • Does not include the learner's full name alongside their photo unless separately agreed

To request that a specific photo be removed, or to update your consent preference at any time, please contact us.

Download the Photo & Media Consent Form (PDF)

6. Who we share information with

Admissions information is processed through EduFeesWeb, our fees and admissions management platform, which acts as an operator on our behalf and is bound to keep your information secure and confidential. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. How long we keep information

We keep admissions and guardian information for as long as reasonably necessary to process applications and maintain accurate school records, and in line with any applicable record-keeping requirements. Photos are kept until consent is withdrawn or the content is no longer in active use on our website or materials.

8. Keeping your information secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including secure staff sign-in to our admin systems and encrypted connections (HTTPS) across this website.

9. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you or your child
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of information we no longer have a valid reason to keep
  • Object to processing, or withdraw consent (e.g. for photos), at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been infringed

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly.
